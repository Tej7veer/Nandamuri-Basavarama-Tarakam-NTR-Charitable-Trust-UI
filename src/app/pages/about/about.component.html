About the Trust

Nandamuri Basavarama Tarakam NTR Charitable Trust was founded with a simple belief: meaningful change starts small and grows through consistent, honest effort.

Our Story

We are a newly registered charitable trust. We haven't run our first program yet — but we've spent months planning how to make the biggest difference with the resources our community can offer.

Our Founders

A small group of volunteers and community organizers came together, motivated by local needs in education, healthcare, and food security.

Our Vision & Mission

Vision: A community where opportunity isn't limited by circumstance.

Mission: To design and deliver transparent, community-driven programs in education, healthcare, food support, environment, and women's empowerment.
